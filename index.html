ABOUT

One Wi-Fi network, zero setup

Drexora Transfer doesn't use mobile data, Bluetooth, or a cloud account. It works entirely over the Wi-Fi network your devices are already connected to — the same one your router hands out at home, in the office, or on a guest network.

When you open the app on your phone and your laptop, each one broadcasts itself on that local network. The moment both are open and connected to the same Wi-Fi, they detect each other automatically — no pairing code, no QR scan, no manual IP entry.

Once detected, a device shows up in the list by its local IP address, ready to receive. If a device leaves the network or closes the app, it disappears from the list just as automatically.

SERVICES

What Drexora Transfer does

Everything it offers is built around one job: moving files between your own devices, fast and privately.

Local Wi-Fi transfer

Files move directly over your Wi-Fi network — no internet connection or mobile data required.

Automatic device discovery

Both apps detect each other the instant they're open on the same network — no codes to enter.

Files and folders

Send single files or full folders in one drop — structure stays intact on the receiving end.

Private by design

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Files stay on your network, between your two devices only.

Transfer history

Every send and receive is logged with a timestamp, so past transfers are easy to find again.

Two-way, one window

Send and receive from the same screen on both Windows and Android.
